**Last updated: 2026-08-31**

## Teza

> Cyber_Folks (CBF, GPW) — hosting/SaaS/telecom group, nie cybersecurity company. Cena ~190–192 zł, −14% od all-time high 222 zł (9.01.2026), ale +14% w rocznych skalach. FY 2025: revenue 855,80 mln zł (+30% YoY), net income 128,30 mln zł. Q1 2026: revenue +29%, adjusted EBITDA +35%, net profit +48% — silny początek roku. Główny growth driver: e-commerce segment (MailerLite, PrestaShop, Sylius, Bitbag) — z 3 mln zł do 204 mln zł w 2025 + nowe akwizycje. AI investment 40 mln zł; management projektuje znormalizowane marże od Q1 2026, EBITDA CAGR +20% 2026-2029. Ryzyka: ATH spadkowy trend, e-commerce dependency, dług netto/equity 51-52%, kompresja marż (net margin 8% vs 16% LY). Katalizator: **2.09.2026** Q2 2026 earnings. Analysts: Strong Buy, target 217 zł (+14%), SWS fair value 336 zł.

## Biznes model

- **Subscription-based SaaS:** WordPress, WooCommerce, PrestaShop hosting; domain registration/transfer; AI site builder
- **E-commerce SaaS:** online store builder, content creation, customized templates (MailerLite, PrestaShop, Sylius, Bitbag)
- **Omnichannel communication:** marketing emails, MarTech, web/mobile push notifications, voice, SMS, RCS
- **TV and internet infrastructure**
- **Geographic:** 100+ countries, 360k+ customers, ~1000+ employees, HQ Poznań (ul. Wierzbiecice 1B)
- **Founded:** 1999 (25 years); formerly R22 S.A., renamed to Cyber_Folks S.A. August 2023
- **IPO:** December 2017 (GPW)
- **CEO:** Jakub Dwernicki; vision: "building a European leader in digital technologies"
- Source: [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/symbols/GPW-CBF/), [cyberfolks.com](https://cyberfolks.com/about-us)
