## Co to jest Cyber_Folks

**To hosting/SaaS/telecom group, nie cybersecurity company.**

- Founded 1999 in Poznań; formerly R22 S.A., renamed August 3, 2023
- IPO December 28, 2017 (GPW)
- CEO: Jakub Dwernicki
- Employees: ~1000+, customers: 360k+, countries: 100+
- Business: WordPress/WooCommerce/PrestaShop hosting, domain registration/transfer, AI site builder, SaaS e-commerce (MailerLite), online store builder, omnichannel communication (marketing emails, push notifications, SMS/RCS, voice), TV/internet infrastructure
- Source: [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/symbols/GPW-CBF/), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/CBF.WA/profile), [cyberfolks.com](https://cyberfolks.com/about-us), [GPW](https://www.gpw.pl/spolka?isin=PLR220000018)
